# Changelog

All notable changes to CardGraph are documented here.
Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versioning: [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Editor.** Konva canvas with selection, multi-select, marquee, drag, resize,
  rotate, snapping guides against the canvas centre/edges and other elements,
  inline text editing on double-click, zoom and pan, and keyboard shortcuts
  (⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z, ⌘D, ⌘0, Delete, arrow nudge, Space to pan).
- **Content panel.** Form-driven editing generated from each template's field
  schema, including a live price-history table: the curve, the percentage, the
  arrow and the dates update as you type.
- **Design panel.** Format switcher (4:5, 1:1, 9:16, 16:9, 4:3, 3:4), light and
  dark themes, a fully editable colour palette (accent, text, muted text,
  positive, negative, panel, overlay, dividers) with presets and a picker,
  backdrop controls (blur, overlay, vignette, zoom) and number/date formatting.
- **Style and Layers panels.** Per-node typography, colour, glow and shadow,
  chart styling, divider and source-mark settings; layer list with visibility
  and locking.
- **Motion.** Four perfectly looping presets — hover, sway, float, tilt — with
  duration, amount and frame rate, plus a live preview on the canvas.
- **Export.** PNG and JPG at 1× or 2×, GIF encoded in a Web Worker, MP4 via
  WebCodecs with a WebM fallback, all rendered through the same engine the
  editor draws with.
- **Presets.** Save a design to IndexedDB and apply it to the next card, with
  optional images and `.cardgraph.json` import/export. Documents autosave.
- **Templates.** Price Trend (up and down), Grade Comparison, Sale Comparison,
  Population Report and Top Sales Duo — one for each reference graphic — each
  in both themes and all six formats.
- **Landing page** with a live template preview, and a template gallery with
  real rendered thumbnails plus a "continue where you left off" list.
- Self-hosted OFL fonts (Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald, Montserrat, Inter, Poppins,
  Space Grotesk, Roboto Mono), ~220 KB total.
- Neutral placeholder card artwork for previews and demos, generated by
  `scripts/render-placeholders.mjs`. No copyrighted card images ship.
- Vitest coverage for chart scales and number/date formatting.
